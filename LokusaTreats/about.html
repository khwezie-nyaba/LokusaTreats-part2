Our Beginning

From a Home Kitchen to Your Doorstep

LokusaTreats began in 2015 as a small stall selling fudge and coconut ice at local markets.

Today, LokusaTreats is a boutique bakery producing cupcakes, cookies, chocolates, and celebration hampers.

We continue to believe that every treat should be made with care, using quality ingredients and thoughtful presentation.
